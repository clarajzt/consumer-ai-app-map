# Consumer AI App Map

**[Open the interactive map](https://clarajzt.github.io/consumer-ai-app-map/)**

978 consumer AI apps and 466 AI devices, filed by the job people hire them for: assistants and search,
creating, connecting and playing, knowing and learning, acting on your behalf, and devices. Chinese and global
products sit side by side.

Each app shows its website traffic for 2026-10, with an arrow where it moved more than 10% in the month. Built 2026-11-13.

## How to read it

- **A curated sample**, not a census of the market and not a ranking.
- **Traffic** is a third-party estimate of website visits. Mobile-first products are understated.
- **Established products** whose sites are mostly not AI use are shown faded and left out of totals and mover lists.
- **Devices** are ordered by research priority, not by sales.
- A missing number is a gap in the record, not a zero.

## Corrections

Is a product missing, misfiled or out of date? [Open an issue](https://github.com/clarajzt/consumer-ai-app-map/issues/new) with one public link.
Founders correcting their own product are welcome.

Product names and third-party figures remain subject to their owners' rights.
